## Brand & Style

SweetTalk is designed to evoke warmth, charm, playfulness, and approachability. The app combines modern minimalism with friendly, tactile warmth, tailored for individuals looking to elevate their romantic connections and conversational confidence. 

The visual narrative relies on soft pastel gradients, approachable typography, rounded card containers, and pill-shaped interactive elements that feel inviting and non-intimidating.

## Colors

The palette centers on soft pastel blues and dreamy light purples, creating a comforting and delightful atmosphere. 

- **Primary:** Soft Indigo / Periwinkle (`#7C83FD`) for key interactive actions and active navigation states.
- **Secondary:** Pastel Blue (`#96BFFF`) for supportive banners, highlights, and secondary tags.
- **Tertiary:** Soft Blush Pink / Lavender (`#FFC6FF`) for warm accents and celebratory highlights.
- **Neutral Base:** Crisp white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`) paired with off-white surface layers (`#F8F9FA`) and deep slate text (`#2D3142`).

## Typography

The typography utilizes Plus Jakarta Sans for its soft, rounded geometry and friendly, optimistic tone. Headlines are bold and grounded, while body text remains clean and highly legible across compact mobile viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

SweetTalk uses a mobile-first fluid grid layout with comfortable 16px screen margins. Content is organized into friendly, floating cards with generous internal padding (16px to 24px) to ensure an uncluttered, breathable interface. Spacing follows a predictable 4px/8px incremental rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through subtle, diffused ambient shadows tinted with primary blue hues, combined with smooth tonal layers. Surface containers use clean white and soft pastel gradients against light neutral backgrounds, avoiding harsh borders in favor of soft, approachable layering.

## Shapes

The shape language is heavily rounded and pill-focused. Cards feature large 24px to 32px corner radii, while buttons, tags, and search inputs utilize fully rounded pill shapes (9999px) to reinforce the friendly, approachable personality of the app.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), featuring soft pastel gradient fills with crisp white label text and subtle elevation on active states.
- **Chips & Tags:** Fully rounded capsule tags used for filtering categories (e.g., "Flirty", "Romantic", "Witty") with soft pastel background tints.
- **Input Fields:** Generously padded, pill-shaped search and prompt inputs with soft inner shadows or light neutral borders and prominent search icons.
- **Cards:** Large, friendly containers with generous corner radii (24px+), soft drop shadows, and internal pastel color blocking for featured generators or daily pick-up lines.
- **Navigation Bar:** Floating bottom bar with rounded pill framing, utilizing clear iconography and active pill highlights for selected tabs.
